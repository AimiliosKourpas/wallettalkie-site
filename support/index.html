Support

Wallet Talkie version 0.1.0. For help, questions or bug reports, email wallettalkie@gmail.com.

Please do not email passwords, authentication codes, card or bank details, or other sensitive financial information. We will never ask you for them. Describe the problem in general terms instead.

Frequently asked questions

Account and sign-in

How do I sign in? Use Sign in with Apple or Google on the first screen. Wallet Talkie does not use passwords. Your sign-in is managed by Apple or Google.

I cannot sign in. Check that your internet connection works and that you are signed in to the right Apple or Google account. Try again after a few minutes. If it still fails, email us with the details listed under "Reporting a problem".

Voice entry

How do I record an entry by voice? Tap the microphone button and say the entry, for example "coffee four euros". Allow microphone and speech-recognition access when iPhone asks.

The wrong amount or name appeared. Speech recognition can make mistakes. Check the draft before you save, and edit it if needed.

Can I turn voice entry off? You do not need to use it. You can always add entries with the keypad.

Deleting your account

How do I delete my account? Open Settings (Ρυθμίσεις) in the app, scroll to the bottom, and choose Delete account (Διαγραφή λογαριασμού). Confirm the prompt. The app removes your account and your records on our server, revokes your Apple authorization where one was issued, and clears account-specific data on your device.

What is not removed? Some device preferences such as theme and App Lock may remain on the device until you remove the app. Apple and Google keep their own records of sign-in under their own policies. See the Privacy Policy for details.

The deletion did not complete. Nothing is deleted if the server cannot complete the process, so you can safely try again later. If it keeps failing, email us.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for what we process, where it is stored and how to exercise your rights. Wallet Talkie has no advertising and no tracking.

Reporting a problem

Email wallettalkie@gmail.com and include, where you can:

  • The app version (shown in Settings, currently 0.1.0).
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • What you were doing, and what you expected to happen.
  • What actually happened, including any message on screen.
  • When it happened, with the approximate date and time.
  • A screenshot, if it shows nothing sensitive. Remove amounts, account names or other personal details first.

Do not include your password, any authentication or verification code, card or bank numbers, or other sensitive financial information.
